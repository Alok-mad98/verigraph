Can you trust this paper's data and references — and what should you study next?

Research assistants summarize papers. VeriGraph asks three sharper questions and answers them with auditable evidence:

  • Extract — turn every figure, table, and chart into structured data a computer can use.
  • Verify — check every citation against Crossref's 150M+ records and flag the fakes.
  • Hypothesize — propose new research questions, each tagged with the evidence it rests on.

Instead of generating another summary, it returns a trustworthiness score (0–100), a per-agent execution trace + handoff graph, a cost table, an estimate of research time saved — plus a downloadable Markdown report.

[ 01 · PROBLEM ]

The two silent bottlenecks in research

Reading a paper isn't the slow part. Transcribing its tables into usable data takes 3–5 hours per review, and a growing share of AI-assisted writing cites papers that don't exist. VeriGraph automates both, then proposes what to investigate next.

▦

Figures → data

Researchers manually copy numbers out of tables and charts. Kimi K2.7's vision reads them directly into typed JSON.

✓

Citations → verdicts

3–13% of LLM citations are fabricated (arXiv 2604.03173, 2026). The verifier checks each against Crossref's 150M+ records.

⚗

Evidence → hypotheses

GLM 5.2 turns verified data + citations into testable questions, tagged SUPPORTED vs SPECULATION.

[ 02 · ARCHITECTURE ]

Three agents. Typed handoffs. Auditable.
